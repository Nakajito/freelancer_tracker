Privacy Policy

Effective: May 11, 2026

1. Introduction

PropoTrack ("we", "our", or "us") is a proposal and client management tool for freelancers. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your information. This policy applies to all users of PropoTrack.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following data, which you provide directly when using PropoTrack:

  • Account — email address and name
  • Proposals — title, platform (Upwork/Fiverr/Freelancer/Workana/LinkedIn/Other), amount, status, job URL, proposal URL, sent date, expected and actual response dates
  • Clients — client name
  • Follow-ups — description, due date, completed status, completion timestamp, notes
  • Time entries — hours logged, description, date, billable flag
  • Proposal templates — name and body content
  • Activity logs — actions taken within the platform (status changes, follow-up completions)

We do not collect payment card data. All financial amounts are stored as decimal values you manually enter.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Manage proposals and track their lifecycle
  • Schedule and track follow-up reminders
  • Log time entries and generate billing summaries
  • Send follow-up digest notifications
  • Generate export files (CSV, JSON, monthly HTML summary)
  • Display dashboard analytics and charts
  • Detect potential duplicate proposals

4. Cookies

PropoTrack uses only the cookies necessary for platform functionality:

Cookie Purpose Duration
sessionid Django session authentication Session
csrftoken Cross-Site Request Forgery protection 1 year
Htmx-Request HTMX progressive enhancement header (not a cookie — processed server-side) Per request

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No third-party analytics or marketing scripts are loaded.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a relational database. In development environments, SQLite is used. In production, PostgreSQL 16 is used. All database connections are secured via environment-configured credentials. Authentication is handled by django-allauth with session-based (not token-based) authentication.

Data is isolated per user account — you can only access your own proposals, clients, time entries, and related records.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time (see Section 7). After account deletion, data is removed from the active database within 30 days.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct or update any inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all data
  • Portability — export your data in JSON or CSV format
  • Objection — object to specific processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at noreply@freelancer-tracker.dabg.dev. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We implement standard security practices including HTTPS enforcement in production, CSRF protection, session security, and per-user data isolation at the database query level. No data is shared with third parties.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
